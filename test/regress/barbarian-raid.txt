# Written blind (round 24, G3b): three barbarian soldiers near Rome and a builder of ours next to the horseman. The raid and the
# civilian-danger lines are spoken when the units come into view (mid-turn) or at the next turn start; J lists them under Threats.
# Scenario barbarian-raid on early-t60.
# covers: **/HubEnemies.lua **/HubEvents.lua **/HubPlaces.lua **/HubAnnounce.lua

load early-t60
scenario barbarian-raid

expect Threats, 1 of \d+ categories, \d+ items?
key Escape -Settle 800
no-lua-errors
