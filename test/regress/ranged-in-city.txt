# Verified in game 2026-10-31. A Crossbowman in Rome (bug-ranged-unit-cannot-fire-in-city): the engine refuses every ranged attack
# of a unit standing in a city center (CanStartOperation false, no targets, with or without enemies), so the reason says that, not
# "no enemy it can target in range".
# covers: **/UnitPanel.lua **/HubTargets.lua
load bug-ranged-unit-cannot-fire-in-city
clear-advisors
lua InGameAccess for _,u in Players[0]:GetUnits():Members() do if GameInfo.Units[u:GetType()].UnitType=="UNIT_CROSSBOWMAN" then UI.DeselectAllUnits() UI.SelectUnit(u) end end
wait Crossbowman -Timeout 10
action RangedAttack -Settle 1500
expect ^Cannot ranged attack: a unit inside a city cannot ranged attack, step out first
action Attack -Settle 1500
expect ^Ranged attack\. A unit inside a city cannot ranged attack, step out first\. .* to cancel
key Escape -Settle 800
no-lua-errors
