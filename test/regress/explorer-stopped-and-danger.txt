# An explorer the game offers for orders has stopped exploring (said once, with the danger next to it), and an exploring recon unit near an enemy gets the danger line.

# (SR lines of a lua step show up with the next key: the Home presses)
# covers: **/HubPlaces.lua **/HubEnemies.lua

lua InGameAccess HubPlaces.OnOperationAdded(0, 1638401, 0, GameInfo.UnitOperations.UNITOPERATION_AUTOMATE_EXPLORE.Index); for _, l in ipairs(HubEnemies.ExplorerLines()) do OutputMessageToScreenReader(l) end

expect ^Your exploring Spearman is [12] tiles? from a Barbarian .*Tab for actions, any order takes it over
lua InGameAccess HubPlaces.OnOperationAdded(0, 1638401, 0, GameInfo.UnitOperations.UNITOPERATION_AUTOMATE_EXPLORE.Index); HubPlaces.CheckExplorers()

expect ^Spearman stopped exploring.*needs orders
lua InGameAccess for _, l in ipairs(HubEnemies.ExplorerLines()) do OutputMessageToScreenReader(l) end
key Home -Settle 500
expect-not Your exploring
