# but the game does not count him as a blocker. N used to say "Unit needs orders: Samori Toure" while Enter warned about an exposed Military Engineer.
# Now the action panel label wins once the 2 s window after a move is over: N says the panel's "Next turn" (+ the General as a plain note, and the
# exposed-unit warning Enter will give), and Enter does what N announced.
# covers: **/HubAnnounce.lua **/InGameAccess.lua **/HubEnemies.lua
# no-share-load: ends the turn
load bug-n-samori-vs-enter

action KeyNavWhatsNext -Settle 1500
expect-not (?i)needs orders
expect ^Next turn
expect Samori .* has moves left
expect Enter warns first: Military Engineer exposed

expect Military Engineer exposed: .* Enter again ends the turn anyway
action KeyNavWhatsNext -Settle 1500
expect-not (?i)warns first
key Enter -Settle 4000
expect (?i)turn ended
no-lua-errors
